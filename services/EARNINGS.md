# Earnings scenarios (estimates, not guarantees)

Assumptions: Fiverr keeps 20%; prices rise after the first ~10 reviews; owner time ~30 min per order.
Figures are net of the Fiverr fee, before withdrawal fees and tax.

| Month | Pessimistic | Realistic | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $0 | ~$50 (2 orders × $30) | ~$200 |
| 2 | ~$50 | ~$220 (6 × $45) | ~$500 |
| 3 | ~$150 | ~$440 (10 × $55) | ~$1,000 |
| 6 | ~$400 | ~$1,300 (18 × $90) | ~$3,000 |
| 12 | ~$800 | ~$2,200 (25 × $110) | ~$5,000+ (direct clients, retainers) |
| **Year 1 total** | **~$4,000** | **~$14,700** | **~$35,000+** |

Owner time at the realistic month-12 level: 25 orders × 30 min ≈ 12–13 hours/month.
Biggest levers: first 5–10 reviews, reply speed, repeat/direct clients (no 20% fee), monthly retainers.
