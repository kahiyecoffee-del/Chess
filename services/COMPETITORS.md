## Patterns that top sellers share
1. "Message me before ordering, free scope check" in almost every gig.
2. Clearly scoped packages (rows, pages, slides, hours).
3. Problem-first opening line ("You opened the file, stared at thousands of rows…").
4. Delivery extras: change log, walkthrough video, post-delivery support.
5. Two price tiers in the market: budget ($10–50) with volume, premium ($100–600) with certification and reviews.

## Our positioning
Start in the budget-to-mid band to win the first reviews, differentiate on verified totals and a written change log, then move toward the premium band (c_thompson_it's model) after ~10–20 five-star reviews.
